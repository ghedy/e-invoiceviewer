Invoice senders

By default, only invoices with a declared sender in the pec.fatturapa.it domain are considered, including inside postacert.eml.

The sender field can be forged. This filter does not authenticate certified email or detect malware. Turning it off lets you view forwarded invoices and invoices from other senders. Digital signatures are not verified. The same content limits always apply.
